Lenovo (联想)

Shanghai | Computer Vision

SenseTime (商汤科技)

SenseTime is one of China's leading AI companies — a full-stack platform spanning computer vision, deep learning and multimodal foundation models. Its SenseCore AI infrastructure and SenseNova (日日新) large model series power applications across smart auto, smart city, healthcare, AR/content and generative AI, offering a window into how a national-scale AI platform is built in China.

Key stats: SenseCore infra · SenseNova foundation models

Hangzhou | Cloud AI / E-commerce

Alibaba Group (阿里巴巴)

Alibaba is a global e-commerce giant with significant investments in AI, operating over 10 proprietary large language models (Tongyi Qianwen series) and deploying AI to support more than 1 billion global users across its platforms. The company's DAMO Academy leads cutting-edge AI research.

Key stats: 10+ LLMs · 1B+ users · DAMO Academy

Hangzhou | Robotics

Unitree Robotics (宇树科技)

Unitree Robotics is one of the world's leading manufacturers of consumer-grade quadrupedal robots. The company is especially known for its agile dog-like robots and has recently expanded into humanoid robotics, combining dynamic motion control with affordability.

Key stats: World's #1 consumer quadruped robots

Shenzhen | AI Infrastructure / Telecoms

Huawei (华为)

Huawei's Bantian headquarters is one of the world's largest technology campuses. The company is a cornerstone of China's AI infrastructure — Ascend AI chips, the MindSpore framework, Pangu foundation models, and full-stack enterprise AI across cloud, telecoms, and smart industry.

Key stats: Ascend AI · Pangu LLM · MindSpore

Shenzhen | EV / Autonomous Driving

BYD (比亚迪)

BYD is a global leader in electric vehicles and batteries, producing millions of EVs annually and driving innovation in sustainable transportation and energy solutions. The company is increasingly integrating AI into autonomous driving and smart vehicle systems — demonstrated inside a live factory visit.

Key stats: World's #1 EV maker · AI-powered vehicles

Support

FAQ

Answers about the expedition, logistics, visas, and what to expect on the ground.

Who is this expedition designed for?+

Corporate leadership teams, founders, investors, universities, business schools, policy leaders and institutions worldwide that want first-hand exposure to how China builds and deploys AI — and need to translate it into decisions for their own market.

What is included in the package?+

Five-star accommodation with breakfast, domestic travel and transfers, corporate access to AI headquarters, senior bilingual guides and interpreters, technical briefings, welcome and closing dinners, logistics support, and a post-trip contact dossier. For custom programs, inclusions are scoped to your engagement model.

How are the city trips structured?+

Each route is curated around both frontier AI providers and real-world adopters. We select the strongest combination of Chinese innovation hubs for your sector, preferred companies, timing and market context rather than forcing every group into a fixed city list.

Will we only visit AI companies?+

No. The expedition deliberately mixes frontier AI vendors (model labs, platforms, robotics) with enterprises putting AI into production — across financial services, insurance, energy, mining, manufacturing, healthcare, education and public sector — so you see not just what AI can do, but how it is actually being deployed.

Can the program be customised for our industry or organisation?+

Yes. We run fully customised expeditions for business schools, corporate teams, investors and institutions worldwide. Host companies, briefings and use-case deep dives are matched to your sector, objectives and market context.

Are international flights included?+

No, international flights are not included. We recommend arrival and departure airports after the route is confirmed, and coordinate the domestic travel between selected hubs.

Are company visits guaranteed?+

Company visits are subject to confirmation and availability. If a visit becomes unavailable, a comparable alternative is arranged; HQ access is a core part of what we guarantee in kind.

What about visas for China?+

Visa requirements depend on each participant's passport and trip purpose. We provide invitation letters and coordinate application support for the group.

Is this a business trip or a cultural tour?+

This is a professional AI industry immersion with curated cultural experiences integrated. The core agenda focuses on technology learning, corporate access and a take-home playbook for your organisation.

How is language handled?+

Professional business interpreters accompany the group throughout the expedition.

How far in advance should we scope a group program?+

A typical executive education, corporate or institutional expedition takes 12–16 weeks end-to-end — scoping, sector mapping, host negotiations, logistics, pre-briefing and delivery. Tighter timelines are possible for well-defined briefs.
